Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you.

ToneSynth is run by a small team that builds focused audio tools. If you've found a bug, want a waveform or feature added, spotted a frequency that behaves oddly on your device, or just want to tell us how you're using the generator, please get in touch.

Email us at contact@tonesynth.com.

We read every message and usually reply within two to three business days. When you write about a bug, it helps to include your browser, operating system, and what you expected to happen versus what actually did, so we can reproduce it quickly.

ToneSynth is part of the Audio Tools Network. If your question is about a sibling site, the same address can pass it along.
